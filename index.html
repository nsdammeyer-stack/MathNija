0️⃣ The Zero Hero

When numbers end in 0 (like 30 × 40), ignore the zeros! Multiply 3 × 4 = 12, then put both zeros back!

🪄 The 11s Magic Trick

To multiply any 2-digit number by 11 (like 35 × 11), pull 3 and 5 apart, add them (3+5=8), and place 8 in the middle!

35 × 11 = 385

🪓 The Break & Split

Split a number into friendly tens and ones! For 31 × 35: (30 × 35) + (1 × 35) = 1,050 + 35 = 1,085.

31 × 35 = 1,085

⚖️ Double & Halve

If one number ends in 5 and the other is even (like 15 × 24), double 15 to 30 and halve 24 to 12!

30 × 12 = 360

⚡ Square Ending in 5

35 × 35 = 1,225
